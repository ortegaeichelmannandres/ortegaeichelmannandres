## 📊 Featured Projects

> *Note: My portfolio focuses on intentional, independent industry case studies designed to solve real-world business and data quality challenges.*

### 🎨 1. Art Marketplace Valuation & Inventory Analytics
* **Objective:** Built a commercial intelligence dashboard for art market trends, price distribution, and auction inventory analysis.
* **Problem & Context:** Secondary art platforms and auction houses struggle with price transparency and inventory velocity across various artist categories and mediums.
* **Tech Stack:** Python (EDA & Data Normalization), Tableau / Power BI.
* **Key Deliverables:** 
  - Executive overview tracking sales volume, price-per-medium estimates, and high-value lot distribution.
  - Interactive pricing model to assist appraisers in detecting undervalued assets and tracking market trends.

---

### 📈 2. Executive Operations & Team Resource Allocation Dashboard
* **Objective:** Designed an automated operational dashboard connected to team planner data to audit work distribution and sprint velocity.
* **Problem & Context:** Leadership lacked real-time visibility into cross-team capacity, task completion rates, and historic backlog bottlenecks across multiple concurrent projects.
* **Tech Stack:** Power BI, Microsoft Planner / Teams Integration, Excel (Power Query).
* **Key Deliverables:**
  - Automated tracking system displaying real-time project health, sprint progress, and individual workload capacity.
  - Historic trend analysis reducing auditing time and improving cross-team delivery predictability.

---

### 🛡️ 3. Data Quality & Pipeline Audit Suite for Software Assets
* **Objective:** Developed a modular data validation and anomaly detection tool for large-scale digital asset tracking.
* **Problem & Context:** Inconsistent logging formats and missing data points across multi-team workflows were causing reporting errors in production deliverables.
* **Tech Stack:** Python (Pandas data validation), SQL (PostgreSQL audits), Excel Dashboarding.
* **Key Deliverables:**
  - Automated data quality checks identifying missing fields, duplicate records, and schema mismatches before reporting.
  - Error-rate trend board adopted as a reporting standard to ensure 100% data accuracy for stakeholders.
